Ba

Battery and power profile

Charge, power profile and, on Dell laptops, charging controls.

Shows the battery's charge and the power profile. Click either for the panel; right-click to step through the profiles.

On Dell laptops the panel adds the firmware's Quiet and Cool profiles and its charging modes, including your own start and stop levels. It shows only on machines with a laptop battery.

In the bar

It is in the bar from the start, in the right section. To move or remove it, click the SUBCULT mark at the left of the bar, open Bar layout in Settings, edit the list and apply.

Settings

Battery and power profile settings
SettingDefaultMeans
chargeDisplay"icon-percentage"icon or icon-percentage.
profileDisplay"icon-text"off, icon or icon-text.

Set them under the plugin's id in ~/.config/subcult/shell/bar.json:

{
 "widgets": {
  "subcult.battery": {
   "chargeDisplay": "icon-percentage"
  }
 }
}

Cost

No timers. It wakes only when something it follows changes.

No network. It contacts no servers.
