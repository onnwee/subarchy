Plugins / Weather

We

Weather

Conditions in the bar; forecast, sun, moon and tides in the panel.

Shows a mark for the conditions and the temperature. The panel has the forecast, sunrise and sunset, the moon and, near the coast, tides.

Current conditions come from Open-Meteo every 15 minutes on mains power and every 30 on battery; the forecast every 2 hours, or 6 on battery. Nothing is fetched until you set a location in the panel.

In the bar

It is in the bar from the start, in the centre section. To move or remove it, click the SUBCULT mark at the left of the bar, open Bar layout in Settings, edit the list and apply.

Settings

Weather settings
SettingDefaultMeans
location""The place, set from the panel's search.
name""The place name shown.
units"metric"metric or imperial.

Set them under the plugin's id in ~/.config/subcult/shell/bar.json:

{
 "widgets": {
  "subcult.weather": {
   "units": "metric"
  }
 }
}

It contacts 11 servers:

  • api.open-meteo.com
  • geocoding-api.open-meteo.com
  • archive-api.open-meteo.com
  • air-quality-api.open-meteo.com
  • marine-api.open-meteo.com
  • aviationweather.gov
  • api.weather.gov
  • api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov
  • api-iwls.dfo-mpo.gc.ca
  • www.data.qld.gov.au
  • data.weather.gov.hk
