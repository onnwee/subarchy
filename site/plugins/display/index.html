Di

Display

Names the monitor each bar is on.

A monitor mark whose tooltip names the output, so you can tell bars apart with several screens. It also shows on minimal bars.

In the bar

It is in the bar from the start, in the right section, and on minimal bars. To move or remove it, click the SUBCULT mark at the left of the bar, open Bar layout in Settings, edit the list and apply.

Settings

None. It works as it is.

Cost

No timers. It wakes only when something it follows changes.

No network. It contacts no servers.
