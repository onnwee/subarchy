Tailscale

Tailscale status, accounts and machines.

Shows whether Tailscale is connected, with this machine's name and address. The panel turns it on and off, signs in, switches accounts and lists the machines on your tailnet.

It checks every minute on mains power and every five on battery, and hides when Tailscale is not installed.

In the bar

It is in the bar from the start, in the right section. To move or remove it, click the SUBCULT mark at the left of the bar, open Bar layout in Settings, edit the list and apply.

Settings

None. It works as it is.
